Getting Started

  • Tap the + button on the Records tab to add a new expense.
  • Enter the amount, choose a category, pick the date, and add an optional note, then tap Save.
  • Tap any expense in the list to edit it; swipe left on an expense to delete it.
  • View monthly summaries and charts on the Stats tab.

Managing Categories

  • Open Settings > Categories to see all categories.
  • Tap a category to edit its name, color, and icon.
  • Tap Add Category to create your own category.
  • User-created categories can be deleted by swiping left on them. Built-in categories can be edited but not deleted.
  • When a category is deleted, its expenses are automatically moved to Other.

Backup and Restore

Your data is stored only on your device. To back it up or move it to another device:

  • In Settings > Backup, tap Export Backup to save a copy of your database.
  • Tap Import Backup to restore from an exported file. Importing replaces all current data.
  • If a backup contains categories that are not recognized, the affected records are placed under Other so your spending history stays intact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where is my data stored?

A: All data is stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud or shared with anyone.

Q: How do I transfer my data to a new iPhone?

A: Export a backup on the old device, then import it on the new device. The backup file includes your expenses, categories, and settings.

Q: Can I get a category back after deleting it?

A: Deleted categories cannot be restored directly, but you can recreate them in Settings > Add Category and re-assign expenses manually.

Q: Import failed or the file was rejected?

A: Make sure you selected a valid backup file created by the Export Backup feature. The app verifies the file before importing, so other SQLite files without the expected structure are rejected and your current data is left untouched.

Q: What does the “Database Recovery” message mean?

A: The app could not open your saved database, so it started with a new, empty one and kept the unreadable file on your device (named like db-corrupt-20261006-080937.sqlite). Your data was not sent anywhere. If you exported a backup before, import it in Settings > Backup to restore your records; deleting the app removes the kept file.

Known Issues

  • Amounts are entered without a currency symbol. The app uses your device's number format (for example, 1,234.56 or 1.234,56). The selected currency is for display only and does not convert saved amounts.
